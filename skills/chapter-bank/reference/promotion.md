# chapter-bank reference — promotion (📇 durable notes / Claims Hub)

Read this file before ANY promotion. Promotion is confirm-gated end to end.

## The promotion target changed (Jul 10–11 2026 reconfigure)

A stable `#clm-candidate` is promoted to a **📇 durable note**: page titled
`📇 {bare-sentence claim}`, body = **[[Template - Zettel Claim]]** (create the page first via the
gate, then `;;` → "Zettel — Claim"). The old `CLM — {content}` em-dash form and the DG bracket
form `[[CLM]] - {content}` are **legacy**: the Jul 10 conversion renamed the canonical `CLM —`
pages to 📇 sentence titles. **on_create_check must match a proposed note against ALL THREE
forms** (📇 sentence · `CLM —` · `[[CLM]]`) so a near-duplicate can't fork — broken-bracket
`[CLM]]` mints are a known accident class (see book-ops quarantine).

## Template - Zettel Claim — the fields that matter at promotion

`Type:: [[Claim]]` · `Core idea::` (the title sentence, minus the 📇) · `Explanation::` (how it
differs from neighbors) · `Argument::` ([C] fuller reasoning) · `Basis::` (Literature /
Observation / Reasoning) · `Connections::` (2–3 laterals from the pinned vocabulary) ·
`Qualifications::` (guards; any (Claude) phrasing → #rephrase-before-writing + disclosure line) ·
`Possible uses::` (chapter/article/presentation · hub placement) · `Meta::` (Provenance forced ·
`Index:: [[🌿 Evergreen Notes]]` · `Origin::` source page + ((block-ref)) + date).

Pinned relation vocabulary: **Supports · Grounds · Extends · Tensions-with · Refines-into ·
Contrasts-with · Depends on · Guarded-by · Resolves · Related (last resort).** Folgezettel
continuation ("continued by") = `Refines-into::` / `Extends::` — never mint a new relation.

## Evidence and questions

- **Evidence** — `EVD — {content} — {Source}` nodes, linked (`Supports`/`Opposes`) from every
  claim they bear on. Evidence lives once. Two lanes: **literature** (source page / `#Literature`)
  and **your observations** (`#classroom-anchor`).
- **Questions** — `QUE — {content}` pages (the ~36-page existing practice stands).

## Claims Hub = structure note(s)

`Answerable Teaching/Claims Hub` — an **ordered index of 📇 links in argument sequence**, per
chapter plus a master. Distinct from `[[Answerable Teaching/Tag Hub]]` (controlled vocabulary).
The hub *links* claims; each claim *links* its evidence. The **marshalling of claim + evidence
into prose stays a write-time act** — keep aggregation at the output layer, never baked into the
slip-box (Luhmann aggregated only in the finished writing). A thread that ripens toward an
article graduates to its own 📋 structure-note page (embed canonical `Claim::` blocks there).

## Provenance at promotion — `auctor` / `grounded` [HARD]

Every 📇 minted carries one **forced** field, set at the promotion moment:
**`Provenance:: auctor` or `Provenance:: grounded`. No skip.**

- **`auctor`** = root origination, *yours* — your own theoretical move (the taxonomy, the
  origination criterion, the perceptual seams). **Not a gap to fix; these are the contribution.**
- **`grounded`** = the claim stands on cited EVD / literature.

**Distinct from the `(you)` / `(Claude)` tags [HARD].** Those track *who phrased it*; this
tracks *whether the claim originates with you or stands on literature*. A `(you)`-phrased claim
can be `grounded` (your words, Scott's idea); a claim can be `auctor` even after wide reading
(the synthesis is the origination). Never collapse the two axes.

**on_create_check guard [HARD].** `auctor` (the Provenance:: value) and `[[Authorial-Agency]]`
(the classroom concept page) are the **same word at two levels — related, never merged.**

**Payoff.** The field is a meta-level origination map — which spine claims are *yours* (defend
as contributions) vs. which lean on literature. Downstream it doubles as **authorship evidence
for HEP**. Provenance is **proposed then confirmed** — written only on Katherine's OK.
