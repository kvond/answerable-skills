# Life Design: Active Learning Through Play

Katherine's specification, merged from the two documents she wrote (August 2026). Where the second document differed from the first, the second wins. This is the standing source of truth. Treat prior decisions as standing unless she changes them.

---

## Core principle

**Optimize for becoming, not merely doing.**

She is not looking for exercise. She is looking for active play that builds capability. The ideal activity simultaneously develops physical health, confidence, sociability, communication, skill, and enjoyment. Health is the foundation that lets her participate, not the end goal.

Every recommendation must answer "what capability does this help me become?" rather than "how does this fill an evening?"

---

## The capability taxonomy

This is the scoring vocabulary. Four branches:

**Physical** — strength · mobility · balance · coordination · endurance · inflammation reduction

**Social** — warmth · conversation · confidence · community · meeting new people · ease speaking with strangers

**Professional** — consulting presence · presentation skills · storytelling · leadership · confidence in groups

**Creative** — writing · music · voice · Spanish · movement

These are not separate from her work. They make her a better educator, writer, speaker, and consultant. She is three years from retirement and positioning for consulting and professorial work.

---

## How she learns

Through meaningful goals. Information sticks because it serves an immediate purpose. Her examples: teaching before fully mastering something; using AI to accomplish real work rather than taking a course; Spanish through authentic conversation; steel drum by joining an advanced group; ukulele by playing with others; line dancing by getting on the floor.

The cycle: **short focused instruction → immediate authentic use → reflection → repeat.**

She does not enjoy long periods of abstract preparation before participation.

**She strongly prefers immersion.** Two concentrated days over eight scattered one-hour classes. Dance intensive, surf retreat, writing retreat, ukulele camp, Spanish immersion weekend. Once competent, she maintains through regular community participation.

## Activity priorities

**Highest:** line dancing · social dancing · Spanish conversation · ukulele · water volleyball · paddleboarding · beginner surfing · outdoor mobility · outdoor yoga · tai chi · qigong · swimming for enjoyment

**Lower:** walking · hiking · generic fitness classes · lap swimming · exercise without purpose

**Mobility deserves separate note.** It is not stretching. It restores movement quality, reduces stiffness, improves posture, supports neck and knee function, and makes every other activity easier. It is enjoyable because it feels like learning a physical language rather than exercising. It works at home, in the backyard, or in parks.

---

## Physical constraints

Posterior cervical fusion, C3–T1. Knee limitations. Inflammation management. Preference for strength over impact.

Ideal movement: strength · mobility · balance · coordination · rhythm · body awareness — rather than repetitive pounding.

The operational drop list lives in SKILL.md. The short version: no sustained cervical extension, no rotational load through the neck, no inversions, no asymmetric loaded carries, no repetitive impact, no heated rooms, no deep sustained knee flexion, no strength duplication.

## Cost

Strongly favor free, parks, community recreation, libraries, Meetups, low-cost classes, single-event fees. Avoid expensive memberships and high recurring costs unless the value is exceptional.

A low walk-in price is a feature, not just a saving: it means she can quit without sunk cost, which lowers the barrier to trying.

## Standing note on Spanish

No Spanish immersion weekend exists within three hours of Wilmington — confirmed as a negative finding, August 2026. Regional offerings are ongoing weekly or Saturday classes in DC and Northern Virginia; true immersion is abroad or at Middlebury and Concordia, both summer-only.

Her decision (August 2026): **keep Spanish practice with an AI conversation partner, and save in-person Spanish for travel.** Time in the calendar goes to movement and social activity instead. Do not keep proposing local Spanish classes as a substitute; do surface Spanish *cultural* events, Latino festivals, and Spanish-language mass, which serve the social and cultural capabilities without consuming a practice slot.

She travels to Panama. Latin American Spanish, not Peninsular.
