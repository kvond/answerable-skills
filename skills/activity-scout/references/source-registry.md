## Dead ends — do not re-search without cause

- **Whiskey Throttle Bar & Grill**, Newport Gap Pike — closed as of December 2025.
- **Diamond State Line Dance** — on hiatus until 2027, hotel construction.
- **Newark Free Library** — closed for a two-year rebuild, reopening fall 2027. Any Newark library program has moved or paused, including the adult ukulele circle.
- **Contra dance** — none active in Delaware or Chester County within 40 minutes. Nearest are Swarthmore, Broomall, and an 18th-century English country dance first Wednesdays in Chadds Ford (~25 min, 610-431-2359) — that last one is the gentlest traditional form and worth pursuing.
- **Steel drum** — no community group in Delaware. Only for-hire party bands. One call left, UD Community Music School.
- **Spanish immersion weekends** — none within three hours. Confirmed negative finding. See the standing note in life-design.md.
- **Multi-day beginner surf camps** — none run September through March in DE, MD, or NJ. The season ends. Also the highest-risk activity found, for the neck.
- **Cecil County adult social dance** — essentially a blank. Elkton Senior Center runs exercise and crafts only.
- **Feldenkrais and Alexander Technique** — practitioners exist in Delaware but all in Sussex County, ~2 hours. A genuine gap, since Awareness Through Movement is arguably the best-matched modality to her description of mobility.
- **Cancer Support Community Delaware** — excellent free gentle yoga in Wilmington, but eligibility is restricted to people affected by cancer.
