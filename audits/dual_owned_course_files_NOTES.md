# Course files owned by kvond12, stored in school folders

30 August 2026. 110 files, about 100 MB — weekly agendas, activity guides, the
Forensics and A&P VT decks, and the nine MASTERS (TPT) files.

**Leave them.** The first instinct was to move them to the school account, and
Katherine was right that it is the wrong call, for two reasons.

Re-creating a file changes its id. That breaks the `.gdoc` pointers in the
school folders, every link inside a deck or agenda that points at one, and
anything already posted in Schoology. Ownership cannot be transferred from a
personal account to a Workspace account, so a move means re-creation — there is
no version of this that keeps the ids.

And the direction is backwards. Katherine is about three years from retirement.
The account with a clock on it is the school one. A file owned by kvond12 stays
hers afterwards; a file owned by the district does not. For the decks, the
activity guides and the TPT product, personal ownership is the safer side.

**The actual risk this list exists to record:** deleting files from the kvond12
Drive by owner removes them from the school folders they are stored in. That is
what nearly happened. Ownership is not a proxy for "disposable copy" here.

The repo is not a backup for any of this. `*.pptx` and Google-native files are
not in it and cannot be. The only deck copies are `~/deck_work/IMPORT_FINAL/`
on the Mac and the live files on Drive.
