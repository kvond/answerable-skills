# Master Agenda — Answerable Biology

Purpose: the year's operating page — where every deck, material, activity, fiddle, and challenge lives, cycle by cycle, so you always know what you are teaching from and what comes next. Keep this file open during class.

Planning view: [Year Arc](Year_Arc_20_Cycles.md). Calendar placement: Schedule Options. New to the system: [Start Here](../01_Start_Here/Start_Here_How_Answerable_Biology_Works.md). The ecology cycles are documented in full teaching depth in the [Ecology Unit Guide](../04_Ecology/Ecology_Unit_Guide.md).

## How to read this page

- **Slides** is the canonical file for each cycle — the deck filename is the name of record. Paste your own cloud-drive link over each LINK NEEDED.
- **Days** is the standing 3-block rhythm: Day 1 — the most teaching plus a shorter activity; Day 2 — shorter lesson, longer activity or lab; Day 3 — finish, revise, feedback. The per-cycle Days line tells you what fills each block.
- Links to sims, games, and readings live **on the deck slides** — the deck you are projecting already carries them. This page repeats each cycle's days, materials, challenges, videos, and supplies; the only URLs it carries are the Fiddle (the tab you open before students arrive) and the optional videos.
- The year runs in four arcs: **Ecology (Cycles 01–07) → Cells (08–11) → Genetics (12–16) → Evolution & Human Impact (17–20)**. One caution: some decks' slide-1 teacher blocks carry internal "Unit 1–4" labels that are inconsistent ("Unit 4 · Biology" vs "Unit 4 · Evolution") and do not match the TPT unit numbers. Ignore the on-slide Unit N labels; navigate by cycle number and unit name — REVIEW WITH KATHERINE to resolve the labels in the decks.

## The Day-3 loop and grading (same every cycle)

Every cycle ends the same way, so it is written once here:

1. Students reopen their response slides, run the **AI revision prompt** in that slide's speaker Notes (it quotes one thing right back, asks two questions, names one distinction — it never gives the answer), and write the revised answer directly under the first. **Both stay visible — the change is the point.**
2. Students submit the finished deck to the class folder. Completion = both answers present.
3. You batch the finished decks through the feedback step to produce each student's one-page **Conceptual Growth Report** and your **class summary** — the workflow lives in the AI Feedback folder (../10_AI_Feedback/).
4. **Assessment default:** the gradebook entry is completion; proficiency is read from the Growth Report and class summary, not the completion column (../09_Assessment_Proficiency/). Cycle 20 adds one product on top of this — the capstone proposal.

**Supplies:** each cycle below carries a one-line summary; the full per-cycle checklists and the year shopping view live in the **Master Supply System** (../13_Supplies/).

**Fiddles:** the per-cycle Fiddle is the tab you open before the bell — arrival, transitions, early finishers. The full library and extension options: ../08_Fiddles_Extensions/.
