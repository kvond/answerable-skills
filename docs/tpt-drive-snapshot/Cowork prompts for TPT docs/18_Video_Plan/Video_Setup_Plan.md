# Video Setup Plan

Purpose: the complete list of short screen recordings Katherine should make — one operation per video, in recording-priority order — with exactly what each video shows and exactly where each one gets linked in the documentation.

## Ground rules for the whole set

- **One operation per video.** No long getting-started video exists or should. "Start Here" does the orientation job in text; the videos exist to show click-paths that text cannot carry. If a recording wants to cover two operations, it is two videos.
- **Short.** Target 2–5 minutes each. The Claude LMS document already sets the bar: "screen recordings, one per item, most under five minutes."
- **Real data, Cycle 02.** Record every video against Cycle 02 — Ecosystems & Feeding Relationships, the free demonstration unit — so every buyer, including free-unit trial teachers, can follow along in materials they actually have. Use a test-student account and blur or fictionalize any real student names, faces, and grades before publishing.
- **Katherine's own screens.** Her platform is Schoology with the Chrome-extension Drive upload and no API. Most of these videos are the resolution of a `WORKFLOW DEMONSTRATION NEEDED` flag: the click-path is not in her written materials, so the recording is the documentation. Where a video makes her choose between two working routes (per-block vs. per-cycle collection, Drive route vs. file route), the choice she makes on camera becomes the documented practice — note it and update the flagged text after recording.
- **Say the flag out loud, once.** Where a section below rests on build-drafted guidance rather than her practice (GoGuardian, other-platform videos), the video script keeps her caveat: this is one teacher's way, not a product recommendation.
- **After each video is recorded,** replace the matching `WORKFLOW DEMONSTRATION NEEDED` sentence in the flagged document with the video link. The coverage table at the end maps every flag to its video.

Priority logic: Tier 1 is what a buyer must see to run Cycle 1 Day 1 at all (deck in student hands, submission working, navigation). Tier 2 is what the first Day 3 needs (gradebook, batch, reports) — "the first Day 3 arrives fast." Tier 3 is the per-cycle efficiency layer (Claude, reuse, GoGuardian, email). Tier 4 is occasional reporting. Tier 5 is other-platform coverage, conditional.
