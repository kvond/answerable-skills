## The question to ask of the passage as it currently stands

Does the prose give the reader all four — content, Lezir specifically, the class,
the design — **before** it resolves?

If the passage narrates your decision and its outcome, the reader receives your
judgment and not the conditions that produced it. That is the failure mode you
named yourself: it reads as a rag rather than as support.

## Where this sits against the literature

**Lampert, *Teaching Problems and the Problems of Teaching* (2001).** The closest
existing model. She writes her own fifth-grade teaching as simultaneous relations
with content, with each student across a year, with the class, and with the
problem she set. She refuses to resolve them into a sequence.

**Hawkins, "I, Thou, and It" (1967).** Teacher, child, subject matter. The class
as a collective is missing from the triangle.

**Your position.** Both Lampert and Matusov leave the reader at the moment of
judgment — Lampert out of fidelity to what teaching is like, Matusov because
specifying the move would collapse dialogue into method, which his argument
forbids. You are proposing **parameters**: not the move, but the layers to weigh,
the terms that make a vague judgment addressable, and the tradeoff made visible.

[This will take fire from both directions. Dialogic pedagogy readers will read
parameters as method creep. Practitioner readers will ask why you did not just
say what to do. Naming both objections in the chapter is stronger than being
found by them.]

[On Matusov specifically: your account of where his work frustrates you is worth
more in print than a diplomatic version, and it is the paragraph most likely to
get softened in revision. Decide now whether you are writing that critique
directly, because deciding it later means deciding it tired.]

## The Bakhtinian reading of why he spoke

Authoritative discourse arrives finished and asks to be accepted. Internally
persuasive discourse has to be worked into the learner's own words and stays
unfinished.

A fact that resists assimilation cannot be received as authoritative. Legless
snakes contradicts the improvement model he was carrying, so it could not be
swallowed, and the only route through was to speak. That is why a boy who does
not talk in class talked.

Supporting machinery at classroom level: Nystrand, *Opening Dialogue* — authentic
questions (no known answer) and uptake (the teacher's next move takes the
student's utterance as material rather than evaluating it). Your move on the
board was uptake in its strongest form.

## The extension to teachers, and its cost

[Your line, in my phrasing, to be rewritten in yours before it goes anywhere:
a package that positions the adopting teacher as an executor of the design is a
motivation-model curriculum about an agency-model pedagogy.]

This is a real claim with a price. If teacher agency matters on the same grounds
as student agency, a package that yields good lessons from a teacher who never
grasps the reasoning is a compromise, not a success, and you have to say so in
print. That is a slower and less marketable product than a curriculum house
wants. HEP is likelier to take it than a curriculum publisher would be, but it is
a position you would defend, not an aside.

[A reviewer will press the disanalogy. Students are compelled to be present and
are being formed. Teachers chose the work and already hold judgment you are
developing rather than building. Name the disanalogy yourself. The version that
treats teachers as adult learners with existing expertise is more defensible than
the version that treats them as students.]

[Andragogy — Knowles' term, and the right label for the audience. Its claims are
closer to a set of assumptions than a research base, so lean on it for naming and
not as warrant. Your case for teacher agency stands on its own grounds.]
